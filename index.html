Bio

I am a first-year PhD student in the UNITES lab at the University of North Carolina, Chapel Hill. My research interests include multimodal learning, LLMs, and AI4Science.

Currently working under the supervision of Prof. Tianlong Chen at UNC. I have previously worked with Prof. Chenyan Xiong and Prof. Florian Metze at CMU during undergrad.
